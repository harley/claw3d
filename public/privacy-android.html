Your camera stays on your device.

Cloud Claw processes camera frames on this Android device to recognize hand movements. It does not record or upload camera video, photos or hand landmarks. Microphone access is not requested.

Local names and scores

Names and scores stay on this device. One-hand and two-hand leaderboards are stored separately in the app. This offline app has no Internet permission and does not send gameplay diagnostics to a server.

Your choices

Stop the camera in game settings or close the app. Android settings control camera permission. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes local scores. Android cloud backup is disabled. The test app, release app and earlier Tomko prototype each have separate storage; scores are not transferred between them.

# Historical: three-second right-hand hold

Superseded on September 21, 2026 at the user's request to test faster play.
Commit `45676a0` required three continuous seconds of open right-hand evidence,
after 300 ms acquisition, before a 360 ms virtual strike. The button had a
spinning hold rim and the webcam showed a countdown. The current experiment
uses an upward open-hand action with no extra hold; see `docs/PRODUCT.md`.

That commit also introduced anatomical meshes using visually positioned wrists.
Its tests covered timing and wrist positions, but did not establish physical
mesh clearance. User review correctly found joystick and cabinet penetration.
The replacement fits each finger around the actual ball, attaches the grip to
its full rotation, relocates the right rest pose away from the post, and checks
real skinned triangles through the strike and button depression. Earlier green
checks are not evidence that the old hand contact was correct.
